Saadet

One polyphonic synthesizer and effects for the Felucca FM-1. Choose Prophet, FM6, CZ-1 or SID; all keyboard and accepted MIDI notes play that sound.

Playing

The device has no on-screen interface yet. The screen stays blank; the keys play the current sound and MASTER sets the volume. The other buttons and knobs do nothing. Edit the sound and settings in the editor. Hold OCT- and OCT+ for five seconds to enter update mode.

Sounds and recall

The editor browses factory sounds and 384 shared user slots. Native Prophet, FM6 and CZ-1 sounds share that library. SysEx dumps sent to the device are refused until the device can choose their slots. The complete synth and FX state is saved automatically while idle and restored at startup. There are no projects, patterns, songs, drum kits, recording or motion lanes.

MIDI and USB

Choose OMNI or one receive channel and one output channel. Scales, chords, strum, sustain, pitch bend and modulation remain available. MPC scale-pad mode uses Bank H without switching synth tracks. Tempo and MIDI clock drive the arp and clocked effects.

USB capture is the stereo post-FX master before computer playback is added, preventing monitoring feedback. Playback and capture support 16/24-bit USB formats at 44.1 kHz; the common audio path uses the original Q15 scale. The 24-bit format discards the low byte on playback and pads it with zero on capture.

Editor and backups

Open the editor for Sound, FM6, Library and Settings. Settings contains complete Saadet backup and restore. Only Saadet archives are accepted; projects and Melodee backups cannot be restored.

Saadet's saved state uses flash addresses 0xA0000/0xA1000, formerly part of Melodee project A. Saving Saadet can replace that project's data. See the repository README and BUILDING instructions before installing. This local fork has not been validated by physical audition or control/deadline tests.
