Saadet

One polyphonic synthesizer and effects for the Felucca FM-1. Choose Prophet, FM6, CZ-1 or SID; all keyboard and accepted MIDI notes play that sound.

Playing and editing

Every screen has the same three parts. The header is the instrument: tempo, octave shift, HOLD and battery at the left, the sound (or on HOME its engine) at the right. Under it the four knobs, then the page's picture. At the foot the rail names the page, with the pages SELECT turns to either side.

ControlAction
HOMEThe sound: its four tone knobs, its name and source, the scope with the chord you hold, and chips for the scale, chord keys, arp and inserts. Tap again for the chord wheel; hold for Settings.
SELECTOn HOME the tempo; on a page the next or previous page.
PRESETSOn HOME browse sounds; on an engine page jump between its sections.
EDITThe engine's own panel, in sections. EDIT again opens the section map.
ENV / LFOThe engine's envelopes and modulation (LFO also holds the mod matrix). Hold LFO to try knob changes and let go to put them back; OCT+ keeps them.
SEQVoice: polyphony, unison, glide and bend.
ARP / PLAYArpeggiator and tempo; PLAY toggles HOLD (shown in the header).
SCLScale, root, keyboard mapping, chords and strum. Hold for root and scale shortcuts.
FXThree serial inserts, each with its own wet/dry mix. The keyboard continues playing notes while FX is held.
GLOOutput (level, pan, spread, tempo), tuning, MIDI channels and clock, voice budget and weights. Hold for output shortcuts and tap tempo.
SAVEUser slots and the sound browser; hold to undo the last sound load.
REC / ALGORITHMNo action.

Sounds and recall

Browse factory sounds and 384 shared user slots. Native Prophet, FM6 and CZ-1 imports use the shared sound library. The complete synth and FX state is saved automatically while idle and restored at startup. There are no projects, patterns, songs, drum kits, recording or motion lanes.

MIDI and USB

Choose OMNI or one receive channel and one output channel. Scales, chords, strum, sustain, pitch bend and modulation remain available. MPC scale-pad mode uses Bank H without switching synth tracks. Tempo and MIDI clock drive the arp and clocked effects.

USB capture is the stereo post-FX master before computer playback is added, preventing monitoring feedback. Playback and capture support 16/24-bit USB formats at 44.1 kHz; internal rendering and effects use native 24-bit precision independently of the selected USB format.

Editor and backups

Open the editor for Sound, FM6, Library and Settings. Settings contains complete Saadet backup and restore. Only Saadet archives are accepted; projects and Melodee backups cannot be restored.

Saadet's saved state uses flash addresses 0xA0000/0xA1000, formerly part of Melodee project A. Saving Saadet can replace that project's data.
