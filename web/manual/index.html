Saadet

One polyphonic synthesizer and effects for the Felucca FM-1. Choose Prophet, FM6, CZ-1 or SID; all keyboard and accepted MIDI notes play that sound.

Playing and editing

ControlAction
HOMEFour synth knobs, waveform/chord display and sound lane. Tap again for the chord wheel.
EDIT / ENV / LFOEdit the selected engine's tone and modulation.
ARP / PLAYArpeggiator settings; PLAY toggles HOLD.
SCLScale, root, keyboard mapping, chords and strum.
FXThree inserts and three send slots. Hold or double-tap FX for the 27 keyboard punch effects.
GLOLevel, pan, spread and tempo; MIDI receive/output settings and voice safety pages.
REC / SEQ / former track encoderNo musical action.

Sounds and recall

Browse factory sounds and 384 shared user slots. Native Prophet, FM6 and CZ-1 imports use the shared sound library. The complete synth and FX state is saved automatically while idle and restored at startup. There are no projects, patterns, songs, drum kits, recording or motion lanes.

MIDI and USB

Choose OMNI or one receive channel and one output channel. Scales, chords, strum, sustain, pitch bend and modulation remain available. MPC scale-pad mode uses Bank H without switching synth tracks. Tempo and MIDI clock drive the arp and clocked effects.

USB capture is the stereo post-FX master before computer playback is added, preventing monitoring feedback. Playback and capture support 16/24-bit USB formats at 44.1 kHz; this does not change the inherited synthesis precision.

Editor and backups

Open the editor for Sound, FM6, Library and Settings. Settings contains complete Saadet backup and restore. Only Saadet archives are accepted; projects and Melodee backups cannot be restored.

Saadet's saved state uses flash addresses 0xA0000/0xA1000, formerly part of Melodee project A. Saving Saadet can replace that project's data. See the repository README and BUILDING instructions before installing. This local fork has not been validated by physical audition or control/deadline tests.
